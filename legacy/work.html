Inventory Distribution

A central warehouse and many retail shops, running on one multi-tenant platform.

It covers inventory, distribution from the warehouse, sales in the shops, customer credit and business analytics, for warehouse administrators, shop owners and employees.

Decisions

  • Stock moves between places. Orders go through creation, approval, shipment and receipt, and every stock movement is saved in one transaction.
  • Customers buy on credit and pay in parts. Partial payments, debt tracking, FIFO payment allocation, aging reports and an immutable financial ledger.
  • The network in the shops is unreliable. An offline-first PWA: sales and stock changes are saved in IndexedDB, queued, and synced with conflict resolution when the connection returns.
  • Prices change, but old reports must stay true. Weighted-average costing with immutable transaction snapshots, so past sales and profit reports stay accurate.
  • Every action has to be secure and traceable. Role-based authorization, audit logging, multilingual support, analytics dashboards and optimized backend services.

SportBooker

Sports venue booking for players, venue operators and administrators.

A multi-tenant platform serving the Syrian community through Syria Sport, where people manage bookings, schedules and sports facilities. I worked on both the frontend and the backend.

Decisions

  • Many organizations, one platform. I contributed to a multi-tenant architecture that uses subdomains to keep each organization separate while they share one application.
  • Four kinds of users with different rights. Role-based access control and authentication workflows for administrators, operators, staff and players.
  • Three audiences, on desktop and on phones. Responsive interfaces built from reusable React components, with centralized Zustand state.
  • Everything comes from the API. REST integrations for authentication, bookings, scheduling, user management and tenant-specific data.
  • Work on both sides. NestJS services, PostgreSQL database design, migrations and seed data.
